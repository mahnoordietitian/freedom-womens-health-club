About The Club

Weight loss should fit your real life.

Freedom Women's Health Club is a supportive online community created for women who want to lose weight while building habits they can actually maintain.

We focus on practical nutrition, movement, daily habits, consistency and learning how to make healthier choices in everyday life.

You do not need to follow extreme diets or completely change your lifestyle to make progress.

The goal is to help you create a healthier routine that can continue long after the 90-day journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before joining?

Is Freedom a crash diet?

No. The focus is on sustainable weight-loss habits, practical nutrition, movement and consistency rather than extreme restriction.

How long is the program?

The full Freedom journey is structured over 90 days.

Do I need special foods?

No. The focus is on practical food choices that can fit into your normal routine.

Will I receive daily guidance?

Members will follow structured educational content, actions and check-ins throughout the journey.

Is this suitable for everyone?

The club is designed for adult women seeking general weight-loss and healthy lifestyle support. Individual medical conditions may require personalized guidance from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
