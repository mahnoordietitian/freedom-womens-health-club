Welcome to your Freedom journey

This is your space to learn, practice and build habits that support sustainable weight loss. You don't need to be perfect. Your goal is to become more consistent, confident and comfortable with your everyday choices.

How your journey works

Your 90-day journey is divided into three phases. Each phase builds on the previous one. Open one week at a time, follow the guidance inside that week and focus on progress rather than perfection.

1

Complete your profile

Add the information you currently know, such as your name, age, height and starting weight. You can leave current weight, waist and hip measurements blank and add them later when you have them.

2

Start with Week 1

Don't try to complete everything at once. Open the current week and follow the habits and guidance provided for those days.

Open Week 1 →
3

Follow the weekly plan

Each week will have its own focus. Read the guidance, complete the habits and use the information to make practical changes in your normal routine.

4

Track your progress

Your starting measurements give you a reference point. When you have new measurements, return to your profile and add them. Your dashboard will calculate the changes automatically.

5

Focus on consistency

One missed day does not mean you failed. Simply return to the plan and continue. The goal is to build habits that you can continue beyond these 90 days.

Remember

Your dashboard is a tool to help you understand your progress. Numbers are information, not a judgment. Look at changes over time instead of expecting everything to change immediately.
