How to use your dashboard

Follow these steps throughout your 90-day journey. You don't need to do everything at once.

1

Start with your current week

Open the week available to you and follow the instructions provided there.

2

Complete your daily habits

Follow the daily actions for walking, water, sleep, meals and other habits.

3

Check your progress

Keep track of your weight, measurements and habits so you can see your changes.

4

Complete your weekly check-in

Share your progress, challenges and wins during your weekly check-in.

5

Move forward one week at a time

New weeks will become available as you continue through your 90-day journey.

6

Look back at your progress

Your measurements and completed habits will help you see your progress over time.
