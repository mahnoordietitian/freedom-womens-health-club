Understand Your Numbers

What do these measurements mean?

BMI

BMI is a screening measure calculated from your weight and height. It can provide a general picture of weight status, but it does not measure body fat directly or describe your complete health.

IBW Range

IBW means Ideal Body Weight. The range shown here is an estimated reference range calculated from height using BMI values of 18.5–24.9. It is not a personal target that you must reach.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio

Waist-to-hip ratio compares your waist measurement with your hip measurement. It gives information about how body measurements are distributed. Changes over time can be useful when looked at together with other progress measures.

Starting vs Current

Your starting measurements are your original reference point. Current measurements can be added later. The dashboard then automatically shows the difference.
