What do these numbers mean?

BMI compares weight with height and is a general screening measure. It does not directly measure body fat and should not be used alone to judge health.

IBW means estimated ideal body weight. It is calculated here using a common height-based formula for women. It is only a reference number and does not mean that everyone should reach that exact weight.

Waist-to-hip ratio compares your waist measurement with your hip measurement. It provides another way to look at body-fat distribution. It should be interpreted alongside your overall health, measurements and individual circumstances.
