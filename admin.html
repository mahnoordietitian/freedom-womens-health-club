Your 90-Day Journey

Manage the complete member journey from here. Each week can contain its own goal, days, habits, daily instructions, tracking fields and weekly reflection.

How Your Content Works

Week → overall weekly goal and content

Day → daily tasks and habits

Habit → what the member needs to complete

Tracking → member records completion/progress

Reflection → weekly review and measurements

Lessons → separate educational content

90-Day Journey

Add or edit any week of your program. You can later add more weeks without changing this code.

Edit Week

Everything you add here becomes part of this week's member journey.

Days

Add daily sections only where you need them. Week 1 and Week 2 can have Day 1–7, while later weeks can be completely week-based.

Weekly Reflection

Add the questions members answer at the end of the week. You can change these whenever you want.


Danger Zone

Deleting a week removes its content from the journey. Member completion records are kept separately and are not automatically deleted.

Lessons

Lessons remain separate from weekly habits. Add, edit or delete educational lessons whenever you want.

Members

View the member profiles stored in your Freedom Women's Health Club system.
